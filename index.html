Workflow Automation Platform

Connect your tools and automate everyday workflows with Citynip

Citynip is a workflow automation application that helps users connect business tools and Google Workspace services—including Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Google Calendar—to eliminate manual data entry, route notifications, and keep records synchronized.

Explore How Citynip Works Read Privacy Policy

Purpose of Citynip

Citynip gives individuals and teams a visual workflow engine to automate routine operational tasks on schedules or event triggers—without writing custom glue code.

Automated Data Sync

Automatically log incoming form submissions, invoices, and operational updates into structured spreadsheets and databases.

Smart Notifications

Trigger timely email alerts, calendar reminders, and task assignments only when specific business rules are met.

Document & File Routing

Organize reports, attachments, and project files into designated folders with consistent naming and permissions.

How Citynip Uses Google APIs

Citynip only requests the minimum Google OAuth permissions required for the specific automation workflows you configure.

Gmail

Used strictly to send workflow-triggered emails or read incoming messages matching user-defined filters and labels.

Google Sheets

Used to append, update, or look up rows in spreadsheets explicitly selected by the user inside their workflows.

Google Drive

Used to save generated reports, archive attachments, or organize files in user-specified Drive folders.

Google Calendar

Used to create, update, or check calendar events when scheduling automated reminders and bookings.

Legal & Privacy Disclosures

Last updated: November 2026 · Contact: support@citynip.com

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Google account to Citynip via OAuth 2.0, we access only the Google account profile information (name and email address) and specific Google Workspace data (such as Gmail messages, Google Sheets, Google Drive files, or Google Calendar events) explicitly authorized by the scopes you approve.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to execute the automation workflows you create and configure within Citynip. We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, profiling, or training generalized AI/ML models.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Workflow execution logs are retained only as needed for troubleshooting and are automatically purged on a regular schedule. You may revoke Citynip's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or request data deletion by emailing support@citynip.com.

4. Data Sharing & Limited Use

We do not sell or rent your personal or Google user data to third parties. Data is only transferred to third-party services that you explicitly configure as destinations inside your own workflows, or when required by law. Citynip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using Citynip, you agree to comply with these Terms of Service and all applicable laws and regulations, as well as the Google APIs Terms of Service.

2. Acceptable Use & Prohibited Conduct

You may use Citynip only for lawful business and personal productivity automation. You are strictly prohibited from using Citynip or any connected Google APIs to generate, distribute, or facilitate spam, malware, unauthorized scraping, harassment, or AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII).

3. Account Responsibility & Termination

You are responsible for safeguarding your credentials and any third-party API connections configured in your workspace. We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms or Google API policies.
