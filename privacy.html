Part 1: Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Citynip accesses, uses, stores, protects, and shares information—including Google user data—when you use our workflow automation platform at https://citynip.com.

1. Information We Collect & Google User Data Accessed

When you create an account or connect third-party services via OAuth 2.0, Citynip collects only the minimum information required to operate your workflows:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and basic profile details used to authenticate your Citynip workspace.
  • Google Workspace Data (via authorized OAuth scopes): Depending on the integrations you enable, Citynip may access Gmail messages/drafts, Google Sheets spreadsheets, Google Drive files/folders, or Google Calendar events strictly as configured in your active workflows.
  • Operational Logs: Workflow execution timestamps, status codes, and error messages needed to keep your automations reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

Citynip uses your information solely to provide, maintain, and improve the user-facing workflow automation features you explicitly configure. Specifically, we use Google user data only to:

  • Execute automated triggers and actions that you create (such as appending rows to a spreadsheet, sending a notification email, archiving a file, or scheduling a calendar event).
  • Display workflow execution status and troubleshooting diagnostics inside your Citynip account.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Citynip's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data or transfer it to data brokers or advertising platforms.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertisements.
  • We do not retain or use Google Workspace user data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • Human access to Google user data is strictly prohibited except with your explicit consent for support, for security/abuse investigation, or to comply with applicable law.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is transferred to external services only when you explicitly add those services as steps or destinations inside your own workflows, or when required by valid legal process.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS, and OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Execution payload logs are retained only for short-term debugging and are automatically purged on a rolling schedule. We retain your account credentials only while your account remains active.

6. Your Rights, Revocation & Data Deletion

You can disconnect Citynip from your Google account at any time via your Google Account Security Permissions page. To request full deletion of your Citynip account, stored OAuth tokens, and workflow logs, email support@citynip.com—requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.
